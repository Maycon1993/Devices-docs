Devices returned to Microsoft with a missing Solid State Drive (SSD) are subject to a Solid State Drive (SSD) replacement fee unless the device is enrolled in the Drive (SSD) Retention offer.

9. Based on local video playback test. Testing conducted by Microsoft in January 2025 using preproduction software and preproduction Surface Pro for Business, Copilot+ PC | Intel Core Ultra 5 256GB, 16GB RAM (LCD) devices and Intel Core Ultra 7 256GB, 16GB RAM (OLED) 11th Edition devices. Testing consisted of full battery discharge during video playback of a .mov file through the Windows Media Player application in 1080p at 24 FPS. All settings were default except screen brightness set to 150 nits with Autobrightness disabled. Wi-Fi was connected to a network. Battery life varies significantly with settings, usage, and other factors.
