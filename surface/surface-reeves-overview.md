# Surface Reeves technical overview

The Surface Reeves Docking Station is a reliable, cost-effective solution for connecting essential peripherals. It allows you to elevate your desktop setup with essential dock connectivity and productivity. The compact design delivers 65W of power for your device and connected accessories, and fast speeds for optimal performance.

Users will find Surface Reeves beneficial when working in a full desktop setup. They can connect Surface Reeves to dual 4K monitors through USB-C or HDMI, enhancing productivity and multitasking capabilities.

## Key features

- **Ultra-fast connectivity**: Enjoy 40Gbps data transfer via USB4, four times faster than USB 3.2.
- **Power delivery**: Includes a 100W power supply delivering 65W passthrough charging for laptops and accessory charging.
- **Dual screen support**: Supports 2 x 4K monitors with HDMI or USB-C output, enhancing multitasking.
- **Compact and lightweight**: Dimensions of 120mm x 60mm x 15mm and a weight of 206g, with tactile indicators for port wayfinding.
- **Sustainable design**: Made with up to 90% recycled plastics and a focus on carbon reduction, adhering to Microsoft’s sustainability goals.

## Compatibility

Surface Reeves works seamlessly with Surface devices and USB-C/USB4/Thunderbolt™ 4-enabled devices.

### Supported devices:

- Surface Winters
- Surface Bancroft
- Surface Pro, 11th Edition
- Surface Laptop, 7th Edition
- Surface Pro 10
- Surface Laptop 6
- Surface Pro 9 (Intel/Wi-Fi)
- Surface Pro 8

### USB-C compatibility:

When connected via USB-C (USB 3.2), Surface Reeves supports:

- **One external display up to 4K at 60Hz** (when supported by the device and display).
- [Daisy chaining monitors](/surface/surface-dock-whats-new#connect-multiple-monitors-to-devices-without-usb4thunderbolt-4).

For a full list of compatible devices and details, refer to the [USB-C and Fast Charging for Surface](https://support.microsoft.com/surface/usb-c-and-fast-charging-for-surface-d320ab19-e4ed-c36d-7458-7d7aec69d34a) page.

### Incompatible devices:

- Surface Duo and Duo 2
- Surface Hub and Hub 2S
- Surface Studio 2+
- Surface Laptop Studio

## Manageability and security

Surface Reeves Docking Station enhances IT efficiency with robust management and security features:

- **Firmware updates**: Keep your dock up to date seamlessly via automatic updates through Windows Update or with downloadable driver and firmware packs.
- **MAC address passthrough**: Maintain consistent network identity across different docks for easy management in shared workspaces.
- **Wake-on-LAN from Modern Standby**: IT administrators can remotely wake devices connected to Surface Reeves and perform management tasks automatically.
- **Windows Management Instrumentation (WMI)**: Enables remote monitoring and management of firmware, policy settings, and related data for Surface Reeves Docking Stations. For details, see [Manage Surface Dock with WMI](https://learn.microsoft.com/surface/manage-surface-dock-wmi).
- **Centralized support and warranty service**: IT administrators can access direct support through the Surface Management Portal or Surface Support Portal.

## Connections

Surface Reeves is equipped with versatile ports to support modern workflows:

- **1 front-facing USB-A** (USB 3.2 Gen 2, 7.5W)
- **1 front-facing USB-C** (USB4 Gen 3, compatible with Thunderbolt™ 4, video enabled, 7.5W)
- **1 rear-facing USB-C** (USB4 Gen 3, compatible with Thunderbolt™ 4, video enabled, 7.5W)
- **1 rear-facing HDMI™ 2.1**
- **1 rear-facing USB-C for PSU only**
- **1 Ethernet port** (1Gbps)
- **Security lock support** (Kensington compatible)
